## Anime Pilgrimages Are Also About the Goods and Souvenirs

The joy of an anime pilgrimage goes far beyond visiting the real-world settings of your favorite series. Getting your hands on location-exclusive merchandise and collaboration products is another major highlight of any pilgrimage trip. In recent years, local governments and businesses have been actively partnering with anime franchises to create limited-edition items, resulting in a wealth of must-have goods waiting for fans at pilgrimage sites across the country.

In this article, we round up the best local goods and souvenirs available at popular pilgrimage destinations.

## Chichibu Area (Anohana / The Anthem of the Heart)

### Souvenir Shops Near Seibu-Chichibu Station

In Chichibu, the pilgrimage site for "Anohana: The Flower We Saw That Day," collaboration merchandise is readily available at souvenir shops around the station area.

- **Chichibu Jibasanhin Center**: Well-stocked with Anohana collaboration-packaged sweets and stationery
- **Matsuri no Yu (adjacent to Seibu-Chichibu Station)**: Permanent Anohana and Anthem of the Heart goods section
- **Chichibu Shrine**: Pilgrimage commemorative goshuin-cho (stamp books) are popular
- **Top pick**: The Anohana-packaged version of the local confection "Chichibu Mochi" is a go-to souvenir

## Oarai Area (Girls und Panzer)

### An Entire Town Turned Anime Shop

Oarai, Ibaraki Prefecture -- pilgrimage site for Girls und Panzer (GuP) -- is unique in that shops throughout the shopping street display character panels and sell their own original collaboration products.

- **Oarai Maiwai Market**: Extensive selection of GuP goods, including exclusive pin badges and clear files
- **Individual shops along the shopping streets**: Each store creates its own original collab products; some fans aim to visit every single one
- **Oarai Isosaki Shrine**: The sheer number of GuP ema (prayer plaques) is a sight to behold. Omamori charms are also popular
- **Top pick**: Collect character cards exclusive to each shop in a "stamp rally" style pilgrimage

## Hida-Takayama Area (Hyouka)

### Where Traditional Crafts Meet Anime

In Hida-Takayama, the pilgrimage site for Kyoto Animation's "Hyouka," collaborations blend anime with the region's time-honored crafts and confections.

- **Traditional Hida crafts**: Sarubobo (local charm) keychains featuring character-inspired motifs
- **Hida-Takayama local sake**: Sake from breweries that served as models for locations in the series makes a sophisticated adult souvenir
- **Takayama Morning Market**: Fresh local produce alongside occasional seasonal collaboration items
- **Top pick**: "Hida Beef Buns" -- popular both as a street-food snack and a take-home souvenir

## Numazu Area (Love Live! Sunshine!!)

### An Extensive Goods Lineup Through Shopping Street Partnerships

In Numazu, the pilgrimage site for "Love Live! Sunshine!!," shops throughout the local shopping streets offer exclusive products tied to individual Aqours members.

- **Nakamise Shopping Street**: Each store sells original goods themed around a specific member
- **Numazu Port**: Seafood products in collaboration packaging, limited-edition stickers, and more
- **Awashima Marine Park**: The model for a location in the series. Offers its own original goods selection
- **Top pick**: The popular "oshi-katsu pilgrimage" style, where fans visit the shop assigned to their favorite member

## Tips for Securing Limited-Edition Goods

### Research Before You Go -- It's the Key to Success

Pilgrimage-exclusive merchandise is often produced in limited quantities, so preparation is essential for making sure you don't go home empty-handed.

- **Follow official social media accounts**: New product and collaboration announcements hit official accounts first
- **Visit on weekdays**: Popular items tend to sell out on weekends. Weekday mornings are your best bet
- **Check multiple shops**: The same product may have different stock levels at different locations
- **Check for online availability**: Some items can be purchased online. If you miss something in person, don't give up

### Souvenir Selection Tips

- **Separate personal and gift purchases**: Reserve exclusive items for yourself and opt for food souvenirs for friends -- this approach helps manage your budget
- **Check expiration dates on food items**: Always verify the best-by date on packaged sweets before purchasing
- **Choose compact items**: Acrylic stands and clear files are lightweight and easy to carry
- **Prioritize items only available on-site**: If something can be ordered online later, save your in-person budget for true exclusives

Anime pilgrimage souvenirs become treasured keepsakes that are uniquely tied to the places you visited.
