## Experience the World of SPY x FAMILY in Tokyo

"SPY x FAMILY" by Tatsuya Endo is a wildly popular manga and anime series set in a fictional country during a Cold War-era divide. The story follows a spy father, a telepathic daughter, and an assassin mother as they navigate life as a "makeshift family." While the series is set in Ostania, a European-style city modeled after East Germany, numerous spots in Tokyo evoke its world remarkably well.

In this article, we introduce the best pilgrimage and photography spots in and around Tokyo for SPY x FAMILY fans.

## Ministry of Foreign Affairs Area -- Imagining Twilight's Workplace

### The Imposing Architecture of Kasumigaseki

The series' protagonist Loid Forger (code name: Twilight) carries out spy missions tied to diplomacy. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs building in Tokyo's Kasumigaseki district is surrounded by stately Western-style architecture that captures the atmosphere of the government institutions depicted in the series.

- **Location**: 2-chome Kasumigaseki, Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo
- **Access**: Directly connected to Kasumigaseki Station on the Tokyo Metro
- **Highlights**: The imposing exterior of the Ministry building is ideal for photography
- **Note**: Entry into government buildings is restricted. Enjoy photographing the exterior

## Tokyo Tower Area -- A Day Out with Anya

### Tokyo Tower and Shiba Park

The area around Tokyo Tower perfectly captures the feel of a Forger family outing. The view of Tokyo Tower from Shiba Park in particular has a striking resemblance to a European landmark tower.

- **Tokyo Tower**: The observation deck is the kind of spot that would make Anya go "waku waku" (excited)
- **Shiba Park**: A lush green park where you can imagine the Forger family on a stroll
- **Zojoji Temple**: A historic temple with Tokyo Tower in the background -- an excellent photo spot

### Recommended Photography Points

Looking up at Tokyo Tower from Shiba Park Area 4, surrounded by greenery, creates a wonderfully photogenic composition. The nighttime illumination is equally beautiful, offering a completely different atmosphere from daytime.

## Former Iwasaki Residence Gardens -- Channeling Eden Academy

### Experience Eden Academy Vibes at This Meiji-Era Western Mansion

The former Iwasaki Residence Gardens in Taito-ku features a Meiji-era Western mansion designed by Josiah Conder. Its elegant European architecture is reminiscent of the prestigious Eden Academy that Anya attends, making it especially popular among SPY x FAMILY fans.

- **Address**: 1-3-45 Ikenohata, Taito-ku, Tokyo
- **Access**: 3-minute walk from Yushima Station on the Tokyo Metro
- **Admission**: 400 yen for adults
- **Highlights**: The mansion's interior decor and stained glass windows feel like stepping into the series

## Yokohama Yamate Western Houses -- A European Streetscape

### Walking Through the Streets of Ostania

The Yamate district in Yokohama preserves numerous Western-style houses built during the Meiji and Taisho eras, offering a genuine taste of the European streetscapes seen in the series. Best of all, many of these buildings are open for free interior tours.

- **Diplomat's House**: Its name and elegant presence are a perfect fit for Loid's cover identity
- **Bluff No. 18**: A late-Taisho-era Western house with a warm, homey atmosphere
- **Berrick Hall**: A large pre-war Western mansion whose grand hall calls to mind ballroom dance scenes
- **Access**: 8-minute walk from Motomachi-Chukagai Station on the Minatomirai Line

## Akasaka Palace (State Guest House) -- National-Scale Grandeur

Japan's only Neo-Baroque palace, the Akasaka Palace State Guest House, evokes the splendor of the national ceremonies and high-society scenes depicted in the series.

- **Address**: 2-1-1 Moto-Akasaka, Minato-ku, Tokyo
- **Access**: 7-minute walk from Yotsuya Station (JR / Tokyo Metro)
- **Tours**: Reservation required (apply through the official website)
- **Highlights**: The front facade, forecourt, and main garden fountain are must-sees
