## Anime Pilgrimages Make Great Dates

While anime pilgrimages might conjure images of solo travel, they actually make excellent date plans for couples. Visiting beautiful locations together, relaxing at cafes and restaurants along the way, and sharing the emotions of a beloved series -- that's the unique appeal of a pilgrimage date.

In this article, we've handpicked 10 anime pilgrimage spots that are perfect for couples, complete with nearby date-friendly recommendations.

## 1. Enoshima and Fujisawa Area (Rascal Does Not Dream Series)

### The Ultimate Seaside Date Spot

The Fujisawa-to-Enoshima area, the setting of "Rascal Does Not Dream of Bunny Girl Senpai," is blessed with stunning coastal scenery that's tailor-made for a date. Walking along the Shichirigahama coastline where the characters once walked, or watching the sunset from Enoshima together, will create a truly special memory.

- **Recommended spots**: Shichirigahama coastal road, Enoshima Samuel Cocking Garden, Enoshima Sea Candle
- **Date cafes**: bills Shichirigahama (pancakes with an ocean view), Eggs 'n Things Shonan Enoshima
- **Access**: About 55 minutes from Shinjuku to Fujisawa Station on the Odakyu Line

## 2. Kanda Myojin Shrine Area (Love Live!)

### A Power Spot Within Walking Distance of Akihabara

Kanda Myojin, famous as a "Love Live!" pilgrimage site, is also a power spot known for blessings of good romantic fortune. With Akihabara nearby, you can easily enjoy cafe-hopping and shopping after your shrine visit.

- **Recommended spots**: Kanda Myojin main hall, Amazake Yokocho, Akihabara Electric Town
- **Date food**: Amanoya's amazake (sweet rice drink), Kanda Matsuya's soba noodles
- **Access**: About 7 minutes on foot from JR Akihabara Station, or 5 minutes from Ochanomizu Station

## 3. Kyoto School-Trip Area (K-ON!)

### A Kyoto Date Steeped in History and Culture

The Kyoto spots featured in "K-ON!'s" school trip episodes make for a ready-made classic date course. Stroll from Kitano Tenmangu to the Kinkakuji area and soak in Kyoto's timeless charm together.

- **Recommended spots**: Kitano Tenmangu, Kinkakuji, Arashiyama Bamboo Grove
- **Date cafes**: Yojiya Cafe in Arashiyama, Francois Kissaten in Kawaramachi
- **Access**: About 2 hours 15 minutes from Tokyo to Kyoto Station by shinkansen

## 4. Hida-Takayama (Hyouka)

### A Charming Date Through Historic Streets

Hida-Takayama, the setting of "Hyouka" based on Honobu Yonezawa's novel, is a beautiful date destination with its well-preserved old streets and natural scenery. Wander the shopping streets the characters frequented while sampling Hida beef snacks along the way.

- **Recommended spots**: Old Town streets (Sanmachi-suji), Hie Shrine, Miyagawa Morning Market
- **Date food**: Hida beef nigiri sushi, Takayama ramen, mitarashi dango
- **Access**: About 2 hours 20 minutes from Nagoya on the JR Takayama Line

## 5. Takehara (Tamayura)

### Peaceful Hours in a Quiet Seto Inland Sea Town

Takehara, Hiroshima Prefecture, the setting of "Tamayura," is known as the "Little Kyoto of Aki" for its beautifully preserved historic streets. It's the perfect place to leave the hustle behind and enjoy a leisurely stroll together.

- **Recommended spots**: Historic town preservation district, Fummyokaku at Saiho-ji Temple, Taketsuru Sake Brewery
- **Date cafe**: Traditional kominka (old house) cafes nestled within the historic streets
- **Access**: About 1 hour 20 minutes from Hiroshima Station on the JR Kure Line

## 6. Chichibu (Anohana)

### An Emotional Journey Through a Beloved Series

Chichibu, the setting of "Anohana: The Flower We Saw That Day," is a nature-rich area where couples can share the emotional resonance of this beloved series. The view from Chichibu Bridge is particularly moving.

- **Recommended spots**: Old Chichibu Bridge, Jorin-ji Temple, Hitsujiyama Park (shibazakura moss phlox in spring)
- **Date food**: Waraji katsudon, Chichibu soba, maple-based sweets
- **Access**: About 80 minutes from Ikebukuro on the Seibu Limited Express

## 7. Odaiba (Mobile Suit Gundam)

### A Pilgrimage in Tokyo's Most Popular Date District

The life-size Gundam statue at Odaiba is a spot that both anime fans and non-fan couples can enjoy together. After visiting Gundam Base at DiverCity Tokyo, take in the surrounding shopping complexes and Rainbow Bridge night views.

- **Recommended spots**: Life-size Gundam statue, Gundam Base Tokyo, Odaiba Seaside Park
- **Date dinner**: Night-view dining at AquaCity Odaiba's restaurant floor
- **Access**: About 15 minutes from Shimbashi on the Yurikamome line

## 8. Chofu and Jindaiji Area (GeGeGe no Kitaro)

### A Relaxing Date in a Green Temple Town

The Chofu and Jindaiji area, associated with "GeGeGe no Kitaro," offers a surprisingly lush and natural atmosphere right within Tokyo. A lunch of Jindaiji soba followed by a temple grounds stroll is a popular date plan.

- **Recommended spots**: Jindaiji Temple grounds, Kitaro Chaya, Jindai Botanical Garden
- **Date food**: Jindaiji soba, yokai-themed sweets at Kitaro Chaya
- **Access**: About 15 minutes from Shinjuku to Chofu Station on the Keio Line, then about 10 minutes by bus to Jindaiji

## 9. Yokohama Minato Mirai (Bungo Stray Dogs)

### A Date Course with Stunning Harbor Night Views

Yokohama, the setting of "Bungo Stray Dogs," is an area where anime pilgrimage and dating blend seamlessly. A walk along the waterfront at the Red Brick Warehouse and Yamashita Park guarantees a romantic atmosphere.

- **Recommended spots**: Red Brick Warehouse, Yamashita Park, Yokohama Chinatown
- **Date dinner**: Authentic Chinese cuisine in Yokohama Chinatown, Italian dining on Bashamichi
- **Access**: About 25 minutes from Tokyo Station to Yokohama Station, then the Minatomirai Line to Motomachi-Chukagai Station

## 10. Onomichi (Kamichu! and Others)

### A Nostalgic Day in the Hillside Town

Onomichi, Hiroshima Prefecture, is a hillside town that has served as the setting for numerous anime and films including "Kamichu!" The panoramic view of the Seto Inland Sea from Senkoji Park is breathtaking, and walking together along the Cat Trail is a uniquely special experience.

- **Recommended spots**: Senkoji Park, Cat Trail (Neko no Hosomichi), Onomichi Shopping Street
- **Date cafe**: Guesthouse Anago no Nedoko Cafe, Onomichi Roman Coffee
- **Access**: About 1 hour 20 minutes from Hiroshima Station on the JR Sanyo Line

## Tips for a Successful Pilgrimage Date

Here are some tips to help couples make the most of their pilgrimage date.

- **Watch the anime together beforehand**: Sharing the source material doubles the excitement when you arrive at the real locations
- **Build buffer time into your schedule**: Leave room for cafe breaks and shopping
- **Make restaurant reservations in advance**: Popular spots fill up quickly, so booking ahead ensures a smooth experience
- **Have a Plan B for rain**: Keep indoor alternatives like museums or cafes in mind
- **Balance your partner's interests**: Don't make the day exclusively about pilgrimage -- weave in food, shopping, and general sightseeing
