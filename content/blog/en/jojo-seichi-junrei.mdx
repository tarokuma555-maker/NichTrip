## The Deep Connection Between JoJo's Bizarre Adventure and Sendai

Hirohiko Araki's iconic series "JoJo's Bizarre Adventure" is known for its distinct settings across each part. Part 4, "Diamond Is Unbreakable," is set in the fictional town of Morioh, which is modeled after Sendai in Miyagi Prefecture -- Araki's own hometown. Throughout Sendai, you'll find numerous spots that evoke the world of the series.

## Key Pilgrimage Spots in Sendai

### Sendai Station Area

The area around Sendai Station, the gateway to the real-world Morioh, is packed with spots that appear in the series.

- **Sendai Station** - The model for the station Josuke and friends use regularly in Part 4. The pedestrian deck closely matches depictions in the series
- **Jozenji-dori Avenue** - Sendai's iconic tree-lined boulevard with beautiful zelkova trees. Captures the atmosphere of Morioh's streetscape
- **Kotodai Park** - A popular gathering spot for Sendai residents, believed to be the model for parks featured in the series

### Locations Connected to Hirohiko Araki

- **Sendai's central shopping streets** - Sun Mall Ichibancho and Clis Road evoke the shopping district atmosphere from the series
- **Nishi Park** - A renowned cherry blossom viewing spot that represents one model for Morioh's natural landscapes
- **Sendai Castle ruins (Aoba Castle)** - An observation point overlooking all of Sendai. Perfect for imagining the full scope of Morioh

### Thus Spoke Kishibe Rohan Locations

The spin-off series "Thus Spoke Kishibe Rohan" also features Sendai's scenery. The live-action drama adaptation further boosted interest in these filming locations.

- **Sendai Literary Museum** - A fitting literary space for the manga artist Rohan
- **Sendai Mediatheque** - A facility designed by Toyo Ito that's popular with architecture enthusiasts as well

## Part 5 Pilgrimage Sites in Italy

Part 5, "Golden Wind," is set in Italy. An increasing number of fans combine their pilgrimage with a trip abroad.

- **Naples** - The city where Giorno Giovanna's story begins. Naples Station and the downtown streets feel like they've been pulled straight from the series
- **Rome Colosseum** - The World Heritage site that serves as the setting for the climax
- **Venice** - The beautiful canal city where intense Stand battles unfold

## Access Information

### From Tokyo

- **Tohoku Shinkansen** - The "Hayabusa" service reaches Sendai Station from Tokyo Station in as little as 1 hour 30 minutes
- **Highway bus** - Approximately 5 hours 30 minutes from Tokyo Station to Sendai Station (fares start from around 3,000 yen, making it an economical option)

### Getting Around Sendai

- **Loople Sendai** - A sightseeing loop bus. A one-day pass for 630 yen covers all major spots
- **Sendai Subway** - The Namboku and Tozai lines make city travel convenient
- **On foot** - Many pilgrimage sites near Sendai Station are within walking distance, making the area ideal for exploring on foot
