## Introduction

Getting the most out of an anime pilgrimage takes a bit of planning. When you have multiple spots to visit, thinking through the order and transportation options ahead of time can make a huge difference in how much you enjoy the day.

This article walks you through the process of building an efficient pilgrimage itinerary, step by step.

## Step 1: List Your Spots

Start by listing every location you want to visit.

### For a Single Anime

Go through all the known spots for your show and rank them by priority. Search for the anime on [AnimeTrips](/works) to view detailed spot information and maps.

### For Multiple Anime

When combining locations from different shows, grouping titles by area is the way to go. For example:

- **Kamakura area**: Slam Dunk + Rascal Does Not Dream of Bunny Girl Senpai + TARI TARI
- **Akihabara area**: STEINS;GATE + Love Live! + Sword Art Online
- **Chichibu area**: Anohana + The Anthem of the Heart

## Step 2: Optimize Your Route

Once your list is ready, arrange the stops so that you minimize travel distance.

### Group by Geography

Check spot locations on a map and cluster the ones that are close together. The AnimeTrips map feature lets you see the spatial relationship between spots at a glance.

### Choose Your Transportation

- **Train** -- The most efficient option in urban areas. An IC card (Suica/Pasmo) is a must
- **Bus** -- For reaching suburban spots. Check schedules in advance, as buses run less frequently
- **Walking** -- Ideal when multiple spots are clustered near a station. A great way to soak in the local atmosphere
- **Rental car** -- Best for covering multiple areas in one day or reaching locations not well-served by public transit

### Rough Time Estimates

- Time at each spot: **15 to 30 minutes** (including photos)
- Travel between spots: **Check Google Maps beforehand**
- Meal breaks: **About 1 hour** (even better if there's an anime-related restaurant nearby)
