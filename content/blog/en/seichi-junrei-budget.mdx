## Set a Budget and Start Planning

Want to go on an anime pilgrimage but worried about how much it might cost? With some smart planning, you can enjoy a satisfying day trip for as little as 5,000 yen (about $35 USD).

This article presents three sample plans at different budget levels, along with practical tips for keeping transportation and accommodation costs down.

## 5,000 Yen Budget | Day Trip on a Shoestring

### Visit Anime Locations Near Tokyo in a Single Day

Keep transportation costs low and you can have a full anime pilgrimage experience for under 5,000 yen.

**Sample Plan: Chichibu "Anohana" Pilgrimage**

- **Transportation**: Seibu Railway one-day pass (about 1,100 yen)
- **Lunch**: Chichibu specialty waraji katsu-don (about 1,000 yen)
- **Souvenirs**: Collaboration goods and snacks (about 1,500 yen)
- **Miscellaneous**: Drinks and light snacks (about 400 yen)
- **Total**: About 4,000 yen

### Day-Trip-Friendly Anime Locations from Tokyo

There are more day-trip options from Tokyo than you might think.

- **Kamakura / Shonan (Slam Dunk)**: The Enoden one-day pass at 800 yen covers all the key spots
- **Chichibu (Anohana)**: Seibu Railway day passes make it easy and affordable
- **Chofu / Mitaka (GeGeGe no Kitaro / Ghibli)**: A quick ride on the Keio Line
- **Odaiba (various anime)**: The Yurikamome one-day pass at 820 yen lets you loop the whole area

### Money-Saving Tips for Day Trips

- **Use day passes**: One-day rail passes are perfectly suited for pilgrimage hopping
- **Bring your own lunch**: Grabbing a bento at a convenience store before you go saves a lot on food
- **Focus on free spots**: Shrines, parks, and shopping streets have no admission fees

## 15,000 Yen Budget | 1-Night / 2-Day Plan

### Reach Locations That Are Too Far for a Day Trip

Adding an overnight stay opens up anime locations that would be too far to visit and return from in a single day.

**Sample Plan: Hida Takayama "Hyouka" Pilgrimage**

- **Transportation**: Highway bus round trip (Shinjuku to Takayama, about 7,000 yen with early booking)
- **Accommodation**: Guesthouse for one night (about 3,500 yen)
- **Meals**: Two days' worth (about 3,000 yen)
- **Souvenirs and admission**: About 1,500 yen
- **Total**: About 15,000 yen

### How to Save on Accommodation

Accommodation is usually the biggest expense. Here are ways to cut costs.

- **Guesthouses and hostels**: Dormitory beds run 2,000 to 4,000 yen per night
- **Capsule hotels**: 3,000 to 5,000 yen per night in urban areas, with decent privacy
- **Early booking discounts**: Hotel booking sites often offer 30 to 50 percent off for early reservations
- **Weekday stays**: Sunday through Thursday rates are significantly cheaper than Friday and Saturday

### How to Save on Transportation

- **Highway buses**: Half the cost of the bullet train for long distances. Night buses double as free accommodation
- **Seishun 18 Kippu**: During available periods, this pass lets you ride any JR local train all day for about 2,410 yen
- **Budget airlines (LCC)**: Early booking for flights to Kyushu or Hokkaido can get you tickets under 5,000 yen one way
- **Splitting a rental car**: When traveling with friends, driving can be the most cost-effective option

## 30,000 Yen Budget | 2-Night / 3-Day Full Experience

### A Multi-Show Pilgrimage Across Multiple Areas

With more room in the budget, you can combine spots from several anime into one fulfilling trip.

**Sample Plan: Kansai Anime Location Tour**

- **Transportation**: Shinkansen round trip (Tokyo to Kyoto, about 22,000 yen with EX Nozomi early discount)
- **Accommodation**: Business hotel for two nights (about 10,000 yen with early booking)
- **Meals**: Three days' worth (about 5,000 yen)
- **Local transportation**: Buses and trains (about 2,000 yen)
- **Souvenirs and admission**: About 3,000 yen
- **Total**: About 30,000 yen (can be lower with extra effort)

### Sample 3-Day Route

1. **Day 1**: Arrive in Kyoto. Visit K-ON! locations (former Toyosato Elementary School), explore the Uji area
2. **Day 2**: Head to Nara. Visit the Katsuraki-ni-imasu-Honoikazuchi Shrine (Demon Slayer connection), Nara Park. Afternoon: travel to Osaka for Jarinko Chie spots around Tsutenkaku Tower
3. **Day 3**: Tour remaining Osaka and Kobe anime locations before heading home

## Money-Saving Tips That Work at Any Budget

### Smart Strategies Everyone Should Know

These tips apply no matter how much you plan to spend.

- **Travel off-peak**: Avoiding Golden Week, Obon, and year-end holidays alone can slash transportation and hotel costs dramatically
- **Earn points**: Use Rakuten Travel or Jalan to accumulate reward points for your next trip
- **Look for coupons and discounts**: Municipal tourism coupons, student discounts, and JAF member benefits are often overlooked
- **Pack light**: Fewer bags means no coin locker fees and easier movement between spots

### Managing Your Budget on the Road

- **Split your budget into four categories** -- transportation, accommodation, meals, and souvenirs. Decide the allocation before you go to avoid overspending
- **Lock in transportation early**: Many early-bird discounts have deadlines a month out
- **Adjust the souvenir budget last**: Check your remaining funds mid-trip and buy within your means
