## Kansai: An Underrated Destination for Anime Pilgrimages

Compared to the Tokyo area, Kansai tends to get less attention as an anime pilgrimage destination, but Kyoto, Osaka, and Kobe are the settings for a surprising number of titles. The region's historic streetscapes and distinctive culture lend unique charm to the works set here, and the ability to combine pilgrimage visits with world-class sightseeing is what makes the Kansai area stand out.

This article highlights the best pilgrimage spots in each of the three cities, organized by anime title, and wraps up with a 2-day model route for covering the Kansai highlights efficiently.

## Kyoto Anime Pilgrimage Spots

With a thousand years of history, Kyoto's stunning scenery has made it a natural choice as the backdrop for many anime.

### K-ON!: The School Trip to Kyoto

In Season 2 of "K-ON!," produced by Kyoto Animation, the main characters visit Kyoto on their school trip. The real-world locations are faithfully reproduced in the show, and the episode's pilgrimage route has become a fan favorite.

- **Kitano Tenmangu**: Appears in the scene where Yui and friends pay a visit. Also famous as a shrine to the god of learning
- **Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)**: Depicted as part of the classic school-trip itinerary
- **Arashiyama and Togetsukyo Bridge**: An iconic Arashiyama landmark featured prominently in the episode
- **Kyoto Station**: The Shinkansen arrival scene is faithfully recreated

### The Eccentric Family: Shimogamo Shrine and the Streets of Kyoto

"The Eccentric Family" (Uchoten Kazoku), based on the novel by Morimi Tomihiko, is set in Kyoto and follows a tanuki family. Kyoto's famous landmarks appear throughout, and the show's world blends seamlessly with the real city.

- **Shimogamo Shrine (Kamo Mioya Shrine)**: The name of the protagonist, Shimogamo Yasaburo, is derived from this shrine. The Tadasu no Mori forest path captures the show's atmosphere perfectly
- **Minamiza**: The kabuki theater that plays a role in key scenes
- **Eizan Railway**: The line running from Demachiyanagi toward Kurama appears in the show
- **Pontocho**: One of Kyoto's geisha districts, beautifully depicted in the series

### Sound! Euphonium: The Sacred Spots of Uji

Another Kyoto Animation production, "Sound! Euphonium" (Hibike! Euphonium) is set in Uji City, Kyoto Prefecture. Uji Bridge and the Daikichiyama observation deck are among the many charming Uji locations that appear in the show.

- **Uji Bridge**: A landmark that appears repeatedly in the opening and ending sequences
- **Daikichiyama Observation Deck**: A scenic viewpoint made famous by a key scene with Kumiko
- **Keihan Uji Station**: The station seen in daily commute scenes

## Osaka Anime Pilgrimage Spots

Osaka's lively downtown culture and vibrant streetscapes provide the backdrop for a distinct set of anime titles.

### Jarinko Chie: The Downtown Scenery Around Tsutenkaku

"Jarinko Chie," the anime adaptation of Haruki Etsumi's classic manga, is set in Osaka's Nishinari Ward. Walking through the Shinsekai district around Tsutenkaku Tower, you can still feel the Showa-era Osaka atmosphere depicted in the show.

- **Tsutenkaku Tower**: A symbolic presence in the series. The observation deck is worth a visit too
- **Shinsekai district**: The bustling streets lined with kushikatsu shops feel like the series brought to life
- **Janjan Yokocho**: A retro shopping alley that retains its old-school Showa charm

### The Melancholy of Haruhi Suzumiya: Around Nishinomiya-Kitaguchi

Technically set in Nishinomiya City, Hyogo Prefecture, this title is included here because of its easy access from Osaka. Produced by Kyoto Animation, the show features many locations in and around Nishinomiya.

- **Nishinomiya-Kitaguchi Station**: The terminal station that appears frequently in the series
- **Koyoen Station area**: The neighborhood around the school that served as the model for North High
- **Nishinomiya City Central Library**: The model for the library Haruhi and friends use

### TIGER & BUNNY: Osaka's Futuristic Skyline

The hero-action series "TIGER & BUNNY" is set in the fictional city of Stern Bild, and Osaka's skyline is said to be one of its models. The Umeda Sky Building's rooftop garden and other modern architectural landmarks overlap with the show's visual world.

## Kobe Anime Pilgrimage Spots

Kobe, a port city with a cosmopolitan atmosphere, also serves as the setting for a number of beloved anime.

### Grave of the Fireflies: War-Era Kobe

Studio Ghibli's "Grave of the Fireflies" tells the story of two siblings during wartime, set in Kobe. Visiting the locations connected to the film offers a deeply moving experience that renews one's appreciation for peace.

- **Mikage and Sumiyoshi area**: The model for the neighborhood where Seita and Setsuko lived
- **Kobe City Mikage Public Hall**: A building that preserves the history of the area

### Fate/stay night: The Streets of Kobe

The fictional "Fuyuki City" from the "Fate" series is believed to be modeled in part on Kobe. Kobe landmarks like the Port Tower and Kobe Ohashi Bridge appear in the show.

- **Kobe Port Tower**: The model for Fuyuki City's signature tower
- **Meriken Park**: Believed to have inspired seaside scenes in the series
- **Former Foreign Settlement**: The Western-style district matches the atmosphere of the show

## Tips for a Kansai Anime Pilgrimage

Here are some pointers for making the most of your time in the Kansai region.

- **Use transit passes**: Take advantage of the Kansai Area Pass or similar discount passes
- **Ride the Keihan Railway**: For pilgrimages between Kyoto and Osaka, the Keihan line is especially convenient
- **Choose your season wisely**: Kyoto is at its most beautiful during cherry blossom season (April) and autumn foliage (November), but crowds peak at the same time. For a pilgrimage-focused trip, weekdays in spring or fall are ideal
- **Base yourself in Osaka**: Staying around Umeda or Namba gives you easy access to both Kyoto and Kobe
